# Chapter 1 Summary

Three investigators converge on an impossible fact: Maya's GPR shows a complete platform beneath Broadway; Sarah's audit reveals the 1962–1963 documentation is gone; Robert confirms Project 117 was real and suspended in 1963 with no decommissioning order. Three independent sources point to one station erased from official records. They commit to investigating despite CTA resistance.

**Mysteries planted:**
- Undocumented structure (physical platform, no records)
- Vanished records (1962–1963 files systematically absent)
- Missing decommissioning (station suspended but never formally closed)

**Open:** Why suspend? Who ordered record destruction in 1979?

**Character stakes:** Maya (credibility), Sarah (integrity vs. loyalty), Robert (institutional memory questioned)
