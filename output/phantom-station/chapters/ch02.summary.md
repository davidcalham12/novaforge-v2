# Chapter 2 Summary

Robert reveals the regulatory limbo: Project 117 was suspended but never formally decommissioned, creating a legal gap that allowed concealment (Mystery 3 plants). Sarah searches disposal records from 1979. Maya and Sarah locate the sealed tunnel entrance beneath Broadway and find fresh marks—someone has been maintaining the concealment. Construction evidence emerges: 1962 payroll records, newspaper articles, photographs. Institutional pressure escalates. Robert, Sarah, and Maya each receive warnings from their institutions. Maya's firm's legal department requests her GPR report; the copy line includes a CTA attorney. The question isn't whether they're being watched—it's why it matters enough to notice.
